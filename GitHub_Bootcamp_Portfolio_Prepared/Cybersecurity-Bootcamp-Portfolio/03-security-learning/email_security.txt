Paragraph to train fellow employees.

Criminals are more into cyber attacks ,these type of attacks are usually facilitated by malware ( Malicious Software.). I will be talking about one important and most common cyber attacks we all have encountered called phishing. Phishing : a subset of spam email where victims are persuaded that the email is from a legitimate source. Phishing emails are targeted towards a specific individuals or organisations. they persuade victims to provide login credentials for their online banking ,social media Etc , the list goes on. Being aware of such cyber attacks ( phishing ),can help you not to be at risk in a personal capacity as well as at an organisational level. We should also be aware of social engineering, whereby users are deceived into providing sensitive information or download malware. social engineering in particular requires us to be aware of tactics used by cyber criminals to deceive you. one of the tactics is to get you into an heightened emotional state such as being fearful or excited. This is because you are more likely to rush decisions in a heightened emotional state.
avoid:
clicking on links of malicious websites
visiting a compromised site
inserting infected external hard drives or USB
