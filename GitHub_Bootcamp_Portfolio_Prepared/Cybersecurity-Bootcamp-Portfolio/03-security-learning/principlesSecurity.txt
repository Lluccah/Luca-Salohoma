Principles‑Based Security Governance Framework for Our Consultancy

1.	Governance

The company must have a baseline standard policies and procedures in place Determines how the company makes decisions about security risk management, integrating it with business goals

2.	Tools and devices

All tools and devices, including personal devices must meet the security requirements and standards, they should have up to date antiviruses software

3.	Data protection and Networks

Validating identity and devices is mandatory before access is. Network location being café, Restaurants, or public places such us libraries are strictly prohibited to prevent using public networks.

4.	Policy and Guidelines 

Training employees to understand how to spot threats. Policies with regular training on phishing detection, not to click unknown links and verifying attachments must be in place.

5.	Detections and response

Maintain continuous logging, anomaly detection, alerts to detect unusual login times. Define an incident response plan, so that when a breach occurs, you can response swiftly.
